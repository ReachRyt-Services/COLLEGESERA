COLLEGESERA SEO ADMIN GUIDE
=========================================

1. LOGIN CREDENTIALS
-----------------------------------------
URL:      http://localhost:5173/seo-admin  (or your production URL /seo-admin)
Username: seo
Password: CollegeSera@SEO


2. STEPS AFTER SAVING A CUSTOM TAG
-----------------------------------------
Once you click "Save" on a custom SEO tag (Title, Description, or OG Image) for a specific college in the Admin Panel:

STEP 1: Live Update (Instant)
- The changes are instantly saved to the Supabase database.
- If you visit the specific college page in your browser (e.g. /college/sri-ramakrishna-college-of-arts-and-science), the new SEO title and description are immediately active and visible in the page source (`<head>`).

STEP 2: Update the Sitemap (For Google)
- To ensure Google's crawler gets the absolute latest `sitemap.xml` structure with the new priorities, you need to regenerate the sitemap file.
- Open your terminal in the project folder and run:
  
  npm run sitemap

- This script will fetch the latest SEO data and regenerate `public/sitemap.xml`.

STEP 3: Verify (Optional)
- Open your browser and go to `http://localhost:5173/sitemap.xml` to verify the sitemap has been successfully built.
- Right-click on the specific college page, click "View Page Source", and ensure the `<title>` matches what you entered in the admin panel!
